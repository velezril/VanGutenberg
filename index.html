VanGutenberg LLC

Founded and led by Dr. Ricardo Velez, VanGutenberg LLC is a healthcare consulting firm focused on practice management, revenue optimization, and the strategic expansion of clinical services. We help existing and emerging healthcare organizations plan growth, coordinate operational readiness, and bring new capabilities into service.

We support the development and expansion of neurology offices, psychiatry practices and outpatient mental health services, inpatient mental health programs, diagnostic imaging centers, primary care clinics, chiropractic practices, and other healthcare service lines.

Leadership & Experience

Dr. Ricardo Velez | Founder & Healthcare Consultant

Dr. Ricardo Velez brings a multidisciplinary background in clinical care, healthcare operations, practice management, and organizational improvement. His experience includes managing chiropractic practices across South Florida, supporting healthcare operations, and leading complex planning, organizational development, and performance improvement initiatives. His work bridges clinical operations and business strategy to help healthcare organizations improve service delivery and pursue sustainable growth.

His academic preparation includes a Doctor of Chiropractic degree and graduate education in health law, medical education, and disaster and emergency management. He combines clinical insight with operational strategy to help organizations expand services, improve performance, and navigate implementation challenges.
